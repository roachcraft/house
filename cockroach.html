the "kitten" incident ↘

i debuted as connie-lynn gus; a name i'd been waiting to hatch. however, due to a last-minute need, i was also planned to kitten.

a kitten seemed inappropriate for the apocalypse, so i was a cockroach.

my prepared number wasn't quite what i'd hoped for. but being a cockroach? crawling around, working the crowd, being an absolute creature?

that was wonderful.

so i kept the cockroach.
