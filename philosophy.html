philosophy of a cockroach

leaf 01 / gathered things

i have a habit of bringing things home.

scraps of fabric. discarded words. curious objects. things that caught my attention for reasons i can't always explain.

sometimes i see what something could become. i may have absolutely no idea. often i just find it interesting.

i like looking at familiar things as though i've only just arrived here. before anyone has explained what they're for, what they're worth, or what i'm supposed to think about them.

there's a particular kind of wonder in not knowing any better.

i don't mean that everything is secretly extraordinary. some things really are just garbage. but i like finding out for myself.

once you start questioning what something is for, defining what it is, putting it away in a neat little box; it gets a shitload harder to see what it can be.

leaf 02 / what gets discarded

humans spend an awful lot of time deciding what's worth keeping, what's useful, beautiful, appropriate. what's welcome. what's disposable.

we make similar decisions about creatures, places, & most confusingly, other humans.

i'm not entirely convinced any of us know what the fuck we're talking about.

i've never been especially comfortable with how easily things are thrown away. particularly facing any inevitable waste my own existence produces, in a world already struggling beneath the weight of it.

there's guilt in that. frustration, too, when wanton disregard & explotation is built into the everyday systems we live in.

to be clear: i'm under no illusion that sewing, painting, or hot-gluing garbage together will fix any of this. that's not the tea.

interrupting the process feels worthwhile; picking up something already written off & asking what else it might be.

maybe it isn't finished being something yet.

leaf 03 / making do

a cockroach learns pretty early to make do with whatever is available.

sometimes creativity was play. other times, necessity. more often than not, those were impossible to separate.

as a curious kid with a lot of ideas & not much to work with, i improvised. took things apart. made something out of whatever i could find.

there's nothing to be romanticized about experiencing limited access, but i do think it left me with a particular affection for possibility.

the habit definitely stuck.

somewhere along the way, though, making things became rare & seemingly impossible.

an idea could be examined, questioned, planned, revised, & reconsidered until there was very little chance of it ever becoming an actual thing.

an impressive amount of work could go into making absolutely nothing.

eventually, through the rip tide, i found another way around it.

props to years of therapy too, of course. #gothefucktotherapy

leaf 04 / getting out of my own way

not necessarily an evidence-based method. certainly not a more organized one. just a loophole to begin before i'd managed to talk myself out of beginning.

i've been trying to let the materials do the talking & create intuitively.

starting before i know exactly what i'm making. letting one piece suggest another. allowing things to become other things without insisting that i do or must understand the outcome first.

sometimes that means following an impulse that doesn't make much sense; giving an idea enough room to be stupid.

mostly it means getting the fuck out of the way & allowing a thing agency over its expression.

a grossly under-valued concept, wouldn't you agree?

roachcraft is, in part, a personified workaround for all of this.

i gave the making a name. a verb. a place to live. an identity separate from my own. distance from the spicy part of my brain that wants to hold a committee meeting over every creative idea.

now, there's somewhere for things & moments to happen. just happen. be. at their pace, in their way, simply.

it's a fucking strange system, i know. but more things do seem to be happening.

& i suppose it's no accident that the whole thing was born from a cockroach.

leaf 05 / the cockroach

let us consider the cockroach:

an extraordinarily successful little creature with a remarkably poor reputation.

not particularly sparkly. rarely invited anywhere. frequently misunderstood. exceptionally difficult to get rid of.

there's something deeply appealing about that.

cockroaches persist in circumstances that would be the end of many other creatures. not necessarily through anything spectacular, but through adaptability & a rather inconvenient refusal to stop existing.

there's something all too familiar in being misunderstood, underestimated, or made to feel unwelcome as a poor bipoc afab in the good ol' u.s. of a.

sometimes survival is simply what happens because disappearing isn't an acceptable option.

still here. still poking around. still getting into things. still alive.

cockroaches are resistance & resiliance.
they're punk as hell.

leaf 06 / what freedom!

there's also the rather wonderful matter of expectations.

when someone hears "cockroach", those expectations are generally already on the floor (pun intended).

oh, what freedom!

there's no particular pressure to be impressive, shiny, respectable, or even especially good at anything.

a cockroach is the best excuse to be strange, ridiculous,
to crawl around on the concrete & behind the furniture.

there's room to try something without first establishing whether you're good at it.

& if the whole thing turns out spectacularly stupid, well.

that's jazz 🎵

i think some of that freedom has found its way into the making (the roachcrafting).

when things don't have to become what they're supposed to become, there's a lot more room for them to become something interesting.

once you allow for that kind of nonsense, the line between silly & serious starts getting fabulously blurry.

leaf 07 / further considerations

i make a lot of jokes. some things i make are deeply personal. some ideas are just so profoundly stupid that they really must come to be. other times, making is only an outlet.

things can also mean absolutely nothing.

like anything alive, most are dancing around in some complex symbiosis that's beyond me.

i'm drawn to curiosity, resourcefulness, transformation, & the peculiar things that manage to persist despite everything.

i like to regularly quality-test the limitations folks've been taught to accept, the boundry lines burnt into our retnas by blinding ignorance & fear, including the ones we place on ourselves & each other.

i care deeply about what gets disgarded, what we overlook, & who gets dismissed.

i also think there's enormous value in exercising our free will to be abnormal & covered in glitter.

these things never feel contradictory to me. taking something seriously doesn't mean we have to stop playing with it.

play is exactly what allows us to see something differently in the first place. play keeps gravitas sustainable.

leaf 08 / a work in progress

which leads me to an arguably unhelpful conclusion;

please, for the health of humanity & this giant space rock we're all crawling on, make more ugly, useless, dumb stuff. with your hands. with your particular brain. with what's around. for no reason.

(beyond experimentation, or maybe preserving a precious moment in time)

so many things rarely turn out how they're supposed to.

this is not a problem.
this is the fun of the game.

i will admit, its tough fucking work, keeping this idea in the forefront of an overanalytic mind.

& since a safe place to practice is pretty neccesary,
i built somewhere to do it.

that's what roachcraft is, really.

part collection, part experiment, part state of mind. part evidence that i started something before it fell into a black hole forever.

there are finished things here. unfinished things. things that were meant to be something else. probably things that shouldn't be here at all.

i'm not entirely sure what this place is becoming, but it will inevitably grow a life of its own.

you're welcome to wander, poke around, turn things over, & get lost for a moment.

i'll be doing much the same.

⚠ the house is a work in progress. so am i.

🪳

still here.
